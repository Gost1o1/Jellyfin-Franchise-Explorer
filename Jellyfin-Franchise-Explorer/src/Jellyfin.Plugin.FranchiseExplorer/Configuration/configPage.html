Edit Images

Each image belongs to the currently selected franchise or subgroup. Images are inherited automatically from an associated Jellyfin collection, parent group, or linked media. You can upload a separate image (JPG, PNG or WebP, max 8 MB) to override any individual slot. Click Restore inherited to remove its override. Existing Jellyfin images and media folders remain unchanged.
